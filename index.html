// process

How a project goes down

Simple, structured, and you're never left guessing where things stand.

01

Brief & assets

You send the footage, the references and the vibe. I ask the right questions up front so there are no surprises.

02

Rough cut

First pass at full length. You get a structured cut to react to — this is where the story locks in.

03

Grade & sound

Color, music, sound design, captions, titles — everything polished until the cut feels finished.

04

Delivery

Final export in whatever specs you need, plus your project files if you want them. Ready to post.
